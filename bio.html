Zhuzhu Xie is a research-driven interdisciplinary artist based in London, currently pursuing her masters degree in MFA computational arts at Goldsmiths, University of London. Her practice explores the intersections of culture and technology through mechanical repetition, liminal space, and sensory experience, with a focus on machine agency and performative systems. Grounded in theoretical and material research, she examines how automated processes, and cyclical behaviours allow machines to act beyond human intention, shaping perception and spatial experience. Working across film, photography, sound, installation, and performance, she creates immersive environments that question human-centred control and foreground more-than-human participation.
