This project, “Six-Leg Solution”, explores the aftermath
of the food crisis through a speculative lens. By merging
installation and film, I depict a dystopian world where in-
sects have become humanity’s primary protein source.
The narrative employs engineering terminology with a pos-
itive connotation, creating a stark contrast between text
and visuals. This deliberate juxtaposition raises the ques-
tion: Is this truly the future we desire? The project critiques
the capitalist logic that expansion alone can resolve sys-
temic issues, drawing parallels with the monoculture prob-
lem in industrial agriculture.
